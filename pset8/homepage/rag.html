Robotics, AI & Goals

Robotics and AI are where I turn ideas into reality. Through my experience in VEX Robotics, I learned how to design, build, and think critically under pressure. Now, I’m continuing to explore artificial intelligence and technology, pushing myself to create meaningful and impactful projects. One of my biggest goals is to bring my “Life is a Game” concept to life — combining technology, creativity, and purpose into something that can truly help people.

VEX V5 ROBOTICS

I began my VEX V5 Robotics journey in June 2023 at the International Robotics Academy (IRA) , where I worked as a lead programmer and builder through June 2025. My role focused on developing autonomous routines and drivetrain control systems, as well as contributing to the mechanical design and assembly of competition robots. I implemented efficient control logic, optimized robot performance under time constraints, and collaborated closely with my team to iterate on both hardware and software. This experience strengthened my understanding of systems thinking, real-time problem solving, and the integration of software with physical systems — foundations that continue to shape how I approach engineering and AI today.

As part of Team 1737S, I competed in multiple VEX V5 Robotics competitions at both national and international levels. Our team was recognized with the Design Award at the VEX V5 competition in Azerbaijan for our innovative engineering design and detailed documentation process. At the VEX V5 Nationals, we earned the Sportsmanship Award, reflecting our commitment to collaboration, ethical competition, and teamwork, where we placed in the top 2 out of 40+ teams. We also competed internationally in China, achieving 2nd place, which further demonstrated our ability to perform and adapt in highly competitive environments.

AI & Goals

I’m currently exploring artificial intelligence as the next step in my journey, building on my background in robotics and engineering. I’m especially interested in how intelligent systems can be used to guide decision-making, improve productivity, and create more personalized experiences. One of my main goals is to develop my “Life is a Game” (LG) project — an AI-driven platform designed to help people structure their lives like a game, with clear goals, systems, and progression. The idea is to combine technology, psychology, and design to create something both practical and impactful. As I continue into university, I plan to deepen my knowledge in AI and computer science, and turn this concept into a real, scalable product.
